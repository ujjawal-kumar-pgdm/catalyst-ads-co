FAQ

Questions founders ask us

What ad budget do I need to start?

It depends on your market, offer and goal. We recommend a budget large enough to collect useful data and test messages without starving the campaign.

How soon will I see results?

Campaigns can go live once tracking, feedback and creative are approved. Timing depends on offer readiness and how quickly access is shared.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. We keep engagements flexible and earn renewal with results, communication and clear reporting.

Do you work with my industry?

We work with service businesses, clinics, founder-led brands, agencies and direct-response businesses that need a reliable pipeline.

Who owns the ad accounts and data?

You do. Ad accounts, pixels and creative stay in your name.

What is included in an AI visibility audit?

We test real buyer questions, review whether you are mentioned in AI responses, show where competitors appear and summarise the highest-impact fixes.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) mean making your business easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI to find, trust and mention when people ask questions.

Can you guarantee my business will appear in ChatGPT or Google AI answers?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. AI answers change often. We improve your chances and show you what changed each time, including what did not move.
