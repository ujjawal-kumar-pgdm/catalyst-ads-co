AI Search · AEO & GEO

Show up when customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI

People now ask AI tools "who is the best agency, clinic or shop near me?" and get one short answer. We help make sure the information AI finds about your business is clear, correct and easy to trust. It works for local service businesses and small B2B firms.

1

AI Visibility Audit

We test real buyer questions on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI, then show where you appear, where competitors appear instead, and why.

2

Answer-First Content

Pages and articles written so AI can quote them: a direct answer first, clear headings, real FAQs and plain facts about your offer.

Schema & Technical Setup

FAQ, Organization and Service markup, crawl access for AI bots, clean sitemap and fast pages, so machines can read and trust your site.

4

Authority & Mentions

Hindi & Regional Search

Monthly Citation Tracking

A simple monthly report: which questions mention you, which competitors do, and what we changed. We measure, we do not guess.

What you receive

  • An audit of how AI tools describe your business today, using real questions your customers ask
  • A prioritised list of fixes, written in plain language
  • Updated service pages, FAQs and business information that are clear enough to quote
  • Technical setup: structured data, crawl access and sitemap
  • A report on what changed after each round of work

How we measure it

  • A fixed set of buyer questions, tested on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI
  • Whether your business is mentioned, and whether the details are correct
  • Which competitors and sources are named instead
  • Enquiries from AI search, where you can track them (for example, ask new leads how they found you)

What we cannot promise

  • A ranking, a citation or a mention in any AI tool
  • Identical answers every time, because AI answers change between searches
  • Overnight results. This is steady work on clarity and credibility

FAQ

Questions founders ask us

What ad budget do I need to start?

It depends on your market and goal. We recommend a test budget big enough to collect meaningful data, and we'll size it honestly in your free audit instead of upselling.

How soon will I see results?

Campaigns can go live once the plan, creative and tracking are approved. Timing depends on how quickly access and assets are ready. Early data arrives after the first spend, and we optimise from there. Anyone promising guaranteed results is selling you something.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. We keep engagements flexible and earn the renewal with performance and clear reporting.

Do you work with my industry?

We work with service businesses, startups, founders and small brands. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so in our first chat.

Who owns the ad accounts and data?

You do. Ad accounts, pixels and creative assets stay in your name.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to your ad accounts, your offer and pricing, and a short WhatsApp chat. For AI search work, we also need your service details and a list of questions your customers ask.

What is included in an AI visibility audit?

We test a set of real buyer questions on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI, record whether your business is mentioned and what is said, note who appears instead, and send you a short list of prioritised fixes.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) mean making your business easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, trust and mention when people ask questions.

Is AEO or GEO a replacement for SEO?

No. Good SEO still matters, because AI answers often draw on pages that already rank and are trusted. AEO and GEO build on top of it with clearer answers, structured data and stronger mentions across the web.

Can you guarantee my business will appear in ChatGPT or Google AI answers?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. AI answers change often. We improve your chances and show you the results each month, including what did not move.
